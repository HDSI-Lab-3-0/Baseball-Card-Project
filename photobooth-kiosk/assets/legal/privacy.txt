PRIVACY POLICY

We respect your privacy at this photobooth kiosk.

What we collect:
- Your first name (as entered)
- A selfie photograph
- Motion data (when enabled) used to generate gameplay statistics
- Generated card images

How we use it:
- To create your personalized baseball card and final composite image
- To provide a QR code download link on the local network

Storage:
- Files are saved in a local folder on this machine
- No cloud database is used in this kiosk configuration
- Session files may be deleted by the operator at any time

Sharing:
- We do not sell your data
- Images are not uploaded unless AI stylization is enabled (sent to OpenRouter for processing only)

Your rights:
- You may decline to participate at any time before capture
- Ask staff to delete your session folder after download

Contact the event operator for questions about data handling at this venue.
